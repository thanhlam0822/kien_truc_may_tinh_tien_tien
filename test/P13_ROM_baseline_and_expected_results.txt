Ghi chu kiem thu:
1. InstructionMemory unit test: so sanh dau ra Instruction voi dung 16 dong ROM map o tren.
2. CPU integration test: reset CPU, chay tu PC=0 bang clock; khong nhap truc tiep cac gia tri vao register.
3. Cac ket qua x1-x4 va RAM[0] chi duoc danh dau PASS sau khi quan sat duoc tren mach.
4. Neu nap mot chuong trinh ROM khac, test vector InstructionMemory theo baseline nay se FAIL la dung, vi expected values khong con khop ROM.
5. Day la baseline P13, khong phai chung minh du 25 lenh; cac lenh bat buoc khac can duoc test bang cac chuong trinh ROM rieng hoac lan nap ROM rieng.
